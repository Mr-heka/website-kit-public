## Before authorised publication

- Intended account, project and domain confirmed.
- Costs and external actions authorised where required.
- Public placeholders, false claims and broken contact paths resolved.
- Relevant privacy notice, data minimisation and spam controls reviewed.
- Canonical URL and staging/production indexing checked.
- Rollback reference recorded.

## After publication

Record the live URL, deployed revision, content/asset readback and main-action check.
Separate untested live delivery from successful local tests. If the response to a
write is uncertain, read back first rather than repeating the write.
