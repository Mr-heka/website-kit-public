# Moss & Grain website brief

## Status

This is a fictional website demonstration. Moss & Grain is not presented as a real trading business, and the preview does not collect leads or connect to an enquiry service.

## Business and visitor

- **Visitor:** Households considering whether to repair existing timber furniture.
- **Offer:** Assessment and repair of loose joints and surface scratches, plus refreshes of existing timber finishes.
- **Main action:** “See repair options.”
- **Destination:** The `#repair-options` section on the same page.
- **Scope:** A new one-page website preview using semantic HTML and standalone CSS.

## Verified facts supplied in the brief

- The fictional name is Moss & Grain.
- The studio repairs loose joints.
- The studio repairs surface scratches.
- The studio refreshes existing timber finishes.
- The process starts when a customer shares photos.
- The studio assesses suitability.
- The customer agrees the scope before work begins.
- The intended voice is plain and calm.
- The site must clearly identify itself as a fictional demonstration.

## Available proof and assets

- **Proof:** None supplied. The page uses the stated process to help the visitor understand what happens and does not include testimonials, ratings, guarantees, qualifications, results or customer logos.
- **Assets:** No logo, brand palette, photography or other media supplied.
- **Asset treatment:** The design is typography-led and uses CSS-only abstract timber and joinery details. It makes no claim to show real work, premises, furniture or customers.

## Page outline and draft-copy intent

1. **Demonstration notice:** Makes the fictional status clear before the main navigation.
2. **First screen:** Names the household furniture repair offer, the three service areas and the real on-page action.
3. **Repair options:** Explains loose joint repair, surface scratch repair and timber finish refresh without claiming an outcome that was not supplied.
4. **Suitability principle:** Replaces missing social proof with an honest explanation that the piece and its condition need assessment.
5. **Process:** Presents the supplied three-step path: share photos, assess suitability, agree scope.
6. **Closing action:** Returns the visitor to the repair options and states that no enquiry or booking service is connected.

## Visual direction

The direction is calm, tactile and workshop-inspired without pretending there is an established brand. It uses natural material cues and restrained editorial typography suitable for householders deciding whether an existing piece can be repaired.

- **Palette:** Warm paper `#F2EEE4`, dark ink `#20251F`, moss green `#364F3C`, muted timber `#A96F47`, deep timber `#6C402D`.
- **Typography:** System sans serif for plain explanatory copy; Georgia as an available system serif for large headings. No font downloads.
- **Spacing:** Generous vertical spacing and a maximum content width of 76rem; compact stacks on small screens.
- **Image treatment:** No images. A CSS-only timber panel supports the first screen and is hidden from assistive technology as decoration.
- **Buttons:** Solid moss green with plain rectangular geometry, generous target size and visible focus.
- **Layout:** Responsive layout that stacks on small screens, with asymmetric editorial grids at wider widths.

## Assumptions

- “Studio” describes the supplied fictional concept and does not imply a public premises or location.
- “Households” is the full audience definition because no service area or narrower customer segment was supplied.
- The three supplied service names can be explained only at a general level; no specific technique, material compatibility or result is asserted.
- Internal page navigation is the only complete action because no contact destination was supplied and no lead capture is needed.

## Unresolved launch items

This demonstration is intentionally not launch-ready. A real launch would need:

- a confirmed business identity and approved brand assets;
- a service area;
- approved contact or booking destination if enquiries are required;
- confirmed service detail, limitations and customer questions;
- owned or licensed photography if photographic evidence is desired;
- a canonical production URL and an approved indexing decision;
- any legally required business and privacy information for the final operation.
